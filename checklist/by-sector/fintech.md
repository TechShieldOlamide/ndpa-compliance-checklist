# Sector Add-On: Fintech & Digital Lending

Beyond the [core checklist](../../README.md), fintech and digital lending businesses carry additional exposure because of the volume and sensitivity of financial and identity data involved.

## Additional Obligations

- [ ] BVN, NIN, and other identity verification data is handled with documented, specific safeguards, beyond general "we protect all data" language.
- [ ] Credit scoring or automated lending decisions involving profiling have a documented DPIA, given the higher risk classification of automated decision-making.
- [ ] Data sharing arrangements with credit bureaus, payment processors, or other financial infrastructure providers have signed DPAs (see [core checklist, section 03](../03-breach-vendor-readiness.md)).
- [ ] Compliance overlaps with CBN guidelines are mapped, not treated as a separate, disconnected obligation. NDPA and CBN data handling expectations should be addressed together, not in silos.
- [ ] Debt collection practices involving personal data (especially third-party debt collectors or "shame" tactics using personal contacts) are reviewed for NDPA compliance, this is a known area of regulatory and public scrutiny in Nigerian digital lending.

## Common Gaps Specific to This Sector

- Aggressive marketing use of transaction data without clear, specific consent for that purpose.
- Loan app permissions requesting broad access to contacts, SMS, or photos beyond what's actually needed, a pattern that's drawn direct regulatory attention.
- Data retention long after a loan is closed, with no defined deletion timeline.
