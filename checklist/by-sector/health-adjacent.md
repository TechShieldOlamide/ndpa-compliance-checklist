# Sector Add-On: Health-Adjacent Services

Beyond the [core checklist](../../README.md), businesses handling health-related data (telehealth, wellness apps, diagnostics, insurance-adjacent services) are processing a sensitive data category that carries elevated obligations.

## Additional Obligations

- [ ] Health data is explicitly classified and treated as a sensitive data category, with stricter access controls than general personal data.
- [ ] Explicit, specific consent is obtained for health data processing, general terms-of-service acceptance is not sufficient for this category.
- [ ] A DPIA has been conducted given the elevated risk classification of health data processing (see [core checklist, section 03](../03-breach-vendor-readiness.md)).
- [ ] Access to health data internally is restricted to staff who genuinely need it, not broadly available across the organization.
- [ ] Any sharing of health data with insurers, labs, or other third parties has explicit consent and a signed DPA.

## Common Gaps Specific to This Sector

- Treating health data the same as general customer data in storage and access policy, when it requires a higher standard.
- Consultation notes or diagnostic results stored in general-purpose tools (shared drives, generic CRMs) without access restriction.
- No clear policy on how long health records are retained after a patient/user stops engaging with the service.
