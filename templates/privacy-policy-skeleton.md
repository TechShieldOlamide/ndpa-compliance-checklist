# Privacy Policy Skeleton

This is a structural starting point, not a finished policy. Every section needs to be filled in with what your business *actually* does. A privacy policy that doesn't match your real practices is arguably worse than having none, since it creates a documented gap between what you claim and what you do.

Replace every `[bracketed]` section with your actual details. Delete sections that don't apply. Add sections your business needs that aren't listed here.

---

## Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** [date]

### 1. Who we are

[Business name], [brief description of what the business does]. Contact: [DPO or accountable person's contact details].

### 2. What data we collect

[List every category of data actually collected: name, email, phone number, payment information, delivery address, etc. Be specific, not generic.]

### 3. How we collect it

[Website forms / app signup / WhatsApp / in-person / third-party integrations, name every actual channel.]

### 4. Why we collect it

[For each data category above, state the specific purpose. "To process your order," "to respond to inquiries," "to send marketing communications you've opted into," etc. Avoid vague catch-alls like "to improve our services" without specifics.]

### 5. Legal basis for processing

[Consent / contractual necessity / legitimate interest, whichever applies to each purpose above.]

### 6. Who we share data with

[Name actual third parties: payment processors, delivery/logistics partners, cloud hosting providers, analytics tools. If you use Meta Pixel, Google Analytics, or similar, disclose it here.]

### 7. How long we keep data

[State actual retention periods, not "as long as necessary" with no further detail. If you don't have defined retention periods yet, this is a gap to fix, see the core checklist.]

### 8. Your rights

You have the right to:
- Access the data we hold about you
- Request correction of inaccurate data
- Request deletion of your data
- Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent
- Lodge a complaint with the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC)

[Describe the actual process for exercising these rights: an email address, a form, a documented turnaround time.]

### 9. How we protect your data

[Describe actual security measures in place, not generic claims. Encryption, access controls, staff training, whatever is genuinely in place.]

### 10. Changes to this policy

[How and where updates will be communicated.]

### 11. Contact us

[DPO or accountable contact person, email, and how to reach the NDPC if unsatisfied with your response.]
