# 04. Training & Awareness

Most data breaches and compliance failures trace back to human error, not sophisticated attacks. This is the most consistently under-budgeted area, and it's often the cheapest to fix.

## Staff Training

- [ ] Employees who handle personal data receive training on data protection basics, not just a one-time mention during onboarding.
- [ ] Training is refreshed periodically, not delivered once and assumed to stick.
- [ ] Training covers practical scenarios relevant to their actual role (a customer service rep needs different training than an engineer with database access).
- [ ] New hires receive data protection training as part of onboarding, before they have access to personal data, not weeks after.

**Common gap:** a data protection clause exists in the employee handbook, but no one has actually walked staff through what it means in practice.

## Phishing & Social Engineering Awareness

- [ ] Staff are trained to recognize phishing attempts and social engineering tactics, since these are frequently the entry point for the breaches that lead to data protection failures.
- [ ] There's a clear, low-friction process for staff to report a suspicious email or request without fear of blame.
- [ ] Leadership models good practices, not just requiring it of junior staff.

*Related resource: the [Bank Breach Phishing Readiness Check](https://techshieldolamide.github.io/bank-breach-phishing-check/) is a free tool for assessing personal phishing readiness, useful as a training supplement.*

## Role-Specific Awareness

- [ ] Anyone with elevated data access (admins, engineers, finance) understands the specific data protection obligations tied to their access level.
- [ ] Departing employees have their access revoked promptly, and there's a documented offboarding checklist that includes this step.

## Culture, Not Just Compliance

- [ ] Data protection is discussed as a normal part of how the business operates, not treated as a once-a-year checkbox exercise.
- [ ] Staff feel able to raise data protection concerns without it being seen as slowing things down or being difficult.
