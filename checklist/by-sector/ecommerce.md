# Sector Add-On: E-commerce

Beyond the [core checklist](../../README.md), e-commerce businesses handle a mix of payment, delivery, and marketing data that's often collected through multiple disconnected tools.

## Additional Obligations

- [ ] Payment data flows through a PCI-compliant processor, and you're not storing raw card details yourself.
- [ ] Delivery address and phone number data shared with logistics/courier partners is covered by a DPA (see [core checklist, section 03](../03-breach-vendor-readiness.md)).
- [ ] Marketing consent (email, SMS, WhatsApp) is separate and specific from transactional consent, someone buying a product hasn't automatically agreed to marketing messages.
- [ ] Customer accounts and guest checkout data are both covered by your Records of Processing Activities, not just registered accounts.
- [ ] Any use of customer data for retargeting ads or third-party ad platforms (Meta Pixel, Google Ads) is disclosed and consented to.

## Common Gaps Specific to This Sector

- Adding customers to a WhatsApp broadcast list or newsletter after a single purchase, without explicit opt-in for that specific channel.
- Using multiple disconnected tools (storefront platform, payment gateway, delivery app, spreadsheet for order tracking) with no unified view of where customer data actually lives.
- Reviews and testimonials that include personal details published without the customer's awareness that it's public.
