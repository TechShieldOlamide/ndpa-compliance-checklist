# 06. AI and Automated Decisions

More Nigerian businesses now use AI on personal data, often without calling it AI: credit scoring, fraud detection, customer service chatbots, CV screening, marketing segmentation. The NDPA gives people rights around automated decisions, and regulators increasingly expect you to know how these systems actually behave.

## Know where AI touches personal data

- [ ] You have a list of every tool or model that processes personal data to make or support decisions, including features inside third-party software.
- [ ] For each one, you know what data goes in, what decision or output comes out, and who relies on it.
- [ ] Staff use of public AI tools (pasting customer data into chatbots, for example) is covered by a written policy.

**Common gap:** a vendor switches on an AI feature in software you already use, and nobody updates the records of processing.

## Rights and human review

- [ ] People are told when a decision that significantly affects them is made by automated means.
- [ ] There's a clear way for someone to ask for human review of an automated decision and to contest it.
- [ ] The person doing the review has the authority to change the outcome.

## Risk assessment

- [ ] A DPIA has been completed for AI systems that profile people or make significant decisions about them.
- [ ] The DPIA covers bias and accuracy risks, not only security.

## Testing, not only documentation

- [ ] You've tested that the system behaves the way its documentation, vendor claims and your privacy notice say it does.
- [ ] Testing covers what happens with unexpected inputs, for example whether a chatbot can be tricked into revealing personal data.
- [ ] Results are recorded and repeated after significant changes to the model, its prompts or its data.

**Common gap:** relying on a vendor's description of how a model works, without anyone checking it against real outputs.

## Vendors

- [ ] Contracts with AI vendors say whether your data is used to train their models, and you've chosen the option you're comfortable with.
- [ ] You know where the vendor processes your data, which may be a cross-border transfer (see [05. GAID 2025 Updates](./05-gaid-2025-updates.md)).

---

**Sources:** Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023; NDPA General Application and Implementation Directive 2025; [NDPC official guidance](https://ndpc.gov.ng). For background on testing documented against actual AI behaviour, see [When the Paper Trail Lies](https://github.com/TechShieldOlamide/documented-vs-actual-behavior-paper).
