# Sector Add-On: General SME

If you don't fall into a specific regulated sector, the [core checklist](../../README.md) is your primary reference. This page covers the gaps most common to general small and medium businesses specifically.

## Where General SMEs Most Often Fall Short

- [ ] **"We're too small for this to apply to us."** NDPA obligations aren't purely size-gated. Even small businesses collecting customer names, phone numbers, and emails for basic operations have baseline obligations under section 02 (Data Subject Rights) and should know whether they cross into "major importance" thresholds as they grow.
- [ ] **Customer data scattered across personal devices and tools.** WhatsApp Business, personal email, spreadsheets on someone's laptop, no defined system means no real Record of Processing Activities is possible.
- [ ] **No one has been assigned responsibility.** In small teams, data protection often has no clear owner because everyone assumes it's "handled." See [core checklist, section 01](../01-governance-accountability.md).
- [ ] **Growth outpacing process.** A business that started small and scaled often never revisited its data practices as it grew, meaning today's data volume and today's compliance posture are badly mismatched.

## A Practical Starting Point

If you're a small business trying to figure out where to begin, start in this order:
1. Write down what data you actually collect and where it lives (this alone usually reveals the biggest gaps).
2. Name one person responsible for data protection, even informally, in writing.
3. Draft a real privacy policy, not a template, based on what you wrote in step 1.
4. Work through the [core checklist](../../README.md) in order from there.
