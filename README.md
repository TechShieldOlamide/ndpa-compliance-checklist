# NDPA 2023 Compliance Checklist

A free, plain-language, continuously-updated reference for Nigerian businesses trying to actually understand and meet their obligations under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, not just assume they're covered.

Most compliance content is either a wall of legal text or a marketing page. This isn't either. It's a working checklist, organized by what you actually have to do, with templates you can adapt and links back to primary sources so you can verify everything yourself.

**Maintained by [Olamide Latifat Mohammed](https://olamidelatifat.com)**, Lead Cybersecurity and Compliance Consultant at TrustGrid Technology Limited. Updated as regulations, NDPC guidance, and enforcement patterns evolve. See [CHANGELOG.md](./CHANGELOG.md) for what's changed and when.

Want a scored self-assessment instead of reading the full checklist? Try the [free 10-question NDPA readiness check](https://techshieldolamide.github.io/ndpa-compliance-check/).

## How to use this repo

1. Start with **[Core Checklist](#core-checklist)** below, these four areas apply to every business regardless of sector.
2. If you're in a regulated or high-risk sector, check **[Sector-Specific Add-Ons](#sector-specific-add-ons)** for obligations beyond the baseline.
3. Use **[Templates](#templates)** as starting points, not finished documents. Every business's data flows are different; adapt these to what you actually do.
4. Star or fork this repo if it's useful. Open an issue if something's unclear, wrong, or out of date, corrections make this better for everyone using it.

## A note on scope

This checklist reflects a practical reading of NDPA 2023 and publicly available NDPC guidance as of the last update date in the changelog. It is not legal advice, and it is not a substitute for a formal compliance audit or legal counsel, especially for businesses handling large-scale or sensitive data processing. Where something is genuinely ambiguous in the regulation itself, this repo says so rather than guessing.
