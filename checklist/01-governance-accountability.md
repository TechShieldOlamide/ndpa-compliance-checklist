# 01. Governance & Accountability

The foundation everything else sits on. If you can't show *who* is accountable for data protection and *what* data you actually hold, nothing downstream matters.

## Data Protection Officer (DPO)

- [ ] A DPO, or someone formally designated as responsible for data protection, is named in writing (not just informally understood).
- [ ] This person's contact details are documented internally and, where required, available to data subjects and the NDPC.
- [ ] If your business qualifies as a data controller or processor of major importance (see [NDPC guidance](https://ndpc.gov.ng) for current thresholds), a formal DPO appointment is a legal requirement, not optional.
- [ ] Smaller businesses that don't meet the "major importance" threshold should still name someone accountable. "Nobody owns this" is not a defensible position if something goes wrong.

**Common gap:** businesses that have a privacy policy mentioning a DPO by title, but no actual person has been assigned or informed they hold that responsibility.

## Records of Processing Activities (ROPA)

- [ ] A documented record exists of what personal data you collect, categories of data subjects, purposes of processing, and where/how data is stored.
- [ ] This record is kept current, not created once and forgotten. Review it when you add a new tool, form, or data collection point.
- [ ] The record covers data collected through *all* channels: website forms, physical intake forms, WhatsApp, email, third-party integrations.

**Common gap:** businesses can describe what they do with data in conversation but have never written it down in a form they could hand over if asked.

## NDPC Filing Obligations

- [ ] You've determined whether your business qualifies as a data controller or processor of "major importance" under current NDPC thresholds.
- [ ] If you qualify, your annual compliance audit return has been filed with the NDPC.
- [ ] You're tracking filing deadlines rather than discovering them after the fact.

**Common gap:** businesses genuinely don't know whether this obligation applies to them, and never check.

## Internal Accountability Structure

- [ ] There's a documented escalation path: if an employee has a data protection question or concern, they know who to raise it with.
- [ ] Leadership (not just the DPO) understands their role in data protection decisions, budget, and resourcing.

---

**Sources:** Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023; [NDPC official guidance](https://ndpc.gov.ng). Verify current thresholds and filing deadlines directly with the NDPC, as these are subject to updates.
